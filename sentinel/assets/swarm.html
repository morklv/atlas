COORDINATED MAPPING

Each drone selects a viewpoint expected to reveal currently unknown cells. Reservations discourage choosing the same projected cells in one round; actual measurements can still overlap. Range endpoints update a shared height surface.

STRUCTURAL CHANGE

Pre-incident roof outlines and intact heights are provided as a prior. A measured surface more than 1 m lower becomes a change candidate. This is geometric change detection, not an ML damage classifier.
