WHAT THIS SOLVESGEOMETRY → MOBILITY

Ground: eight-direction A* minimizes horizontal distance plus twice the absolute height change, subject to step, slope and clearance limits. Diagonal moves cannot cut blocked corners.

Air: six-direction 3D A* uses only voxels observed as free. A point cloud alone provides obstacle endpoints, so imported clouds cannot enable aerial routing without visibility evidence.

Routes are candidate geometric paths. There is no vehicle controller, motion smoothing, battery model, live obstacle response, or dispatch connection.
