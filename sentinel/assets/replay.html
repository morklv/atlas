What this demonstration proves—and its limits

This is a replay of recorded sensor, map and planning results. The left scene shows simulator geometry; the planner only receives sensor-derived map evidence. The right map never fills unseen cells from the scene. Motion and yaw are animated between logged poses; the scan and map-reveal effects explain each recorded observation, rather than representing additional camera samples. Green means observed ground, not a certified safe route. The current backend uses known pose and simplified movement; it does not include SLAM, learned perception, or a physical rover controller.
